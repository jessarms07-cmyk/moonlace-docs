# Moonlace FAQ

This page answers common questions about installing, configuring, and using the Moonlace Shopify theme.

## General

### What is Moonlace?

Moonlace is a romantic, editorial Shopify theme designed for boutique and visually driven brands.

It combines flexible storytelling sections with practical shopping features, responsive layouts, product merchandising, and conversion-focused storefront tools.

### What types of stores is Moonlace best for?

Moonlace is especially well suited for:

- Jewelry
- Accessories
- Gifts
- Beauty
- Lifestyle products
- Handmade goods
- Art
- Boutique fashion
- Curated specialty products

It can also be adapted for other visually driven Shopify stores.

### Do I need coding experience to use Moonlace?

No coding experience should be required for normal setup and customization.

Most Moonlace settings are managed through Shopify's visual theme editor.

Advanced customizations beyond the built-in settings may require Liquid, CSS, JavaScript, or help from a Shopify developer.

### Can I preview Moonlace before publishing it?

Yes.

You can customize and preview Moonlace from your Shopify theme library before publishing it as your live storefront theme.

# Mobile and Responsive Design

### Is Moonlace mobile friendly?

Yes.

Moonlace is designed to adapt across desktop, tablet, and mobile screen sizes.

Responsive behavior includes:

- Navigation
- Homepage sections
- Product cards
- Collection controls
- Product pages
- Search
- Cart drawer
- Quick Order
- Footer
- Specialty sections

Always preview your own content on mobile before publishing.

### Why does an image crop differently on mobile?

Desktop and mobile screens use different proportions.

Large storefront images may crop differently depending on available screen space.

For best results:

- Use high-quality images.
- Keep important subjects away from edges.
- Preview both desktop and mobile.
- Use available focal-point or image-position settings where supported.

### Why does text wrap differently on mobile?

Smaller screens provide less horizontal space.

Long headings, buttons, product titles, and menu labels may wrap differently on mobile.

If text becomes difficult to read, shorten the wording or adjust the available settings.

# Products and Collections

### Does Moonlace support product variants?

Yes.

Moonlace uses Shopify's native product and variant system.

Products can include options such as:

- Size
- Color
- Material
- Finish
- Style

### Does Moonlace support sold-out products?

Yes.

Product availability is controlled by Shopify product and inventory settings.

### Can customers filter and sort collections?

Where enabled and configured through Shopify, Moonlace can display collection filtering and sorting controls.

Available filters depend on your store configuration and product data.

### Why is a product missing from a collection?

Check:

1. The product is active.
2. It is available to the Online Store sales channel.
3. It meets the collection conditions.
4. It is assigned to the collection if using a manual collection.
5. Inventory and product availability settings are correct.

# Cart and Checkout

### Does Moonlace include a cart drawer?

Yes.

Moonlace includes a responsive cart drawer that lets customers review and update cart contents without leaving the current page.

### Can customers change quantities in the cart drawer?

Yes.

Customers can adjust quantities, and Moonlace is designed to keep the visible cart count synchronized with Shopify's cart data.

### Can customers remove products from the cart?

Yes.

Products can be removed using the available cart controls.

### Does Moonlace replace Shopify Checkout?

No.

Moonlace controls the storefront theme experience.

Checkout remains Shopify's native checkout system and is controlled by Shopify, the merchant's plan, and store configuration.

# Quick Order

### What is Quick Order?

Quick Order allows customers to add multiple products or quantities from one interface.

It can be useful for:

- Small catalogs
- Repeat purchases
- Sets
- Frequently purchased products
- Wholesale-style ordering
- Customers who already know what they want

### Do I have to use Quick Order?

No.

Quick Order is optional.

If it does not fit your store, you can leave it disabled.

### Does Quick Order work on mobile?

Moonlace is designed for Quick Order to remain responsive on supported mobile screen sizes.

Test your own products and variants before publishing.

# Countdown

### What is the Countdown used for?

The Moonlace Countdown can be used for genuine time-sensitive events such as:

- Product launches
- Sales
- Collection releases
- Events
- Preorder deadlines
- Seasonal promotions

### Does the Countdown automatically restart?

Moonlace is intended to use merchant-configured dates.

Do not use the Countdown to create fake urgency, repeated false deadlines, or misleading scarcity.

For a new promotion, configure a new legitimate date.

# Age Verifier

### Does Moonlace include age verification?

Yes.

Moonlace includes an optional Age Verifier.

### Is the Age Verifier enabled automatically?

No.

Merchants can enable it when appropriate.

### Does the Age Verifier remember visitors?

Moonlace includes remembered verification behavior according to its configuration.

When testing, you may need to use a private browser window or clear browser storage to see the verifier again.

### Does the Age Verifier guarantee legal compliance?

No.

Moonlace provides storefront functionality, not legal advice.

Merchants are responsible for determining whether age verification is required and whether their store complies with applicable rules and laws.

# Apps

### Does Moonlace work with Shopify apps?

Moonlace is built for Shopify's theme system and may work with many apps.

However, compatibility with every third-party app cannot be guaranteed.

Apps may modify:

- Product forms
- Cart behavior
- Search
- Navigation
- Customer accounts
- Product recommendations
- Styling
- JavaScript
- Storefront layout

### What should I do if an app causes a problem?

Try:

1. Confirm the issue began after installing or changing the app.
2. Temporarily disable the app's storefront integration if possible.
3. Test Moonlace again.
4. Contact the app developer if the problem originates from the app.
5. Contact Moonlace support if the issue appears to come from the theme.

# Troubleshooting

### My changes are not appearing. What should I check?

Confirm:

1. The changes were saved.
2. You are viewing the correct theme.
3. You are editing the correct template.
4. You are not previewing an older theme copy.
5. Your browser is not displaying an older cached page.

### My cart is behaving strangely. What should I do?

Try:

1. Refresh the storefront.
2. Test another product.
3. Test in a private browser window.
4. Check recently installed cart apps.
5. Review recent custom code.
6. Contact support if the issue can be reproduced in an unmodified Moonlace theme.

### A section disappeared. What should I check?

Check:

- Correct template
- Section visibility
- Required content
- Selected products or collections
- Whether the section was accidentally removed
- Whether you are editing another theme copy

### Moonlace looks different from the demo store. Why?

The demo store uses its own:

- Products
- Images
- Navigation
- Text
- Collections
- Theme settings
- Merchandising choices

Installing Moonlace provides the theme features and design system, but your store content and configuration will be your own.
