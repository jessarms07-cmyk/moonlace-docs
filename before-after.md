# Before & After Slider

Moonlace includes a Before & After Slider section for visual comparisons.

This can be useful for:

- Beauty results
- Restoration work
- Product transformations
- Art processes
- Design comparisons
- Cleaning or refinishing
- Before-and-after demonstrations

## Add the Section

To add the Before & After Slider:

1. Open Shopify admin.
2. Go to **Online Store > Themes**.
3. Find Moonlace and click **Customize**.
4. Open the page or template where you want the comparison to appear.
5. Click **Add section**.
6. Select the **Before & After Slider** section.
7. Add the two comparison images.
8. Configure any available labels, headings, or supporting text.
9. Save.

## Choose Your Images

For the cleanest comparison, use images with similar:

- Dimensions
- Aspect ratio
- Camera angle
- Subject position
- Framing
- Lighting

The closer the two images are in composition, the easier it is for customers to understand the comparison.

## Image Quality

Use high-quality images that remain clear on both desktop and mobile.

Avoid:

- Very small images
- Heavy compression
- Mismatched image proportions
- Important content placed too close to the edges
- Images containing unnecessary embedded text

## Labels

Where supported, labels can help identify each side of the comparison.

Common examples include:

- Before / After
- Original / Restored
- Untreated / Finished
- Before Use / After Use

Keep labels short and clear.

## Using the Slider

Customers can interact with the slider to reveal more or less of either image.

The section is designed to adapt to different screen sizes.

After setup, test:

- Dragging or adjusting the slider
- Keyboard interaction where applicable
- Touch interaction on mobile
- Image alignment
- Labels
- Mobile layout

## Mobile Testing

On mobile, confirm:

- Both images remain clear.
- The slider control is reachable.
- The comparison does not overflow horizontally.
- Labels remain readable.
- Important image content is not excessively cropped.

## Accessibility

When using comparison imagery:

- Add meaningful alt text where available.
- Do not rely on the images alone to communicate essential information.
- Provide supporting text when the comparison needs explanation.
- Maintain readable labels and sufficient contrast.
- Test keyboard interaction where supported.

## Avoid Misleading Comparisons

Before-and-after content should accurately represent the product, service, or process being shown.

Avoid using:

- Unrelated images
- Manipulated results presented as genuine
- Misleading lighting changes
- Different subjects presented as the same subject
- Comparisons that exaggerate a product result

Use clear supporting information when context matters.

## Troubleshooting

### The Images Do Not Align

Check:

- Both images use similar dimensions.
- Subjects are positioned similarly.
- The images have similar aspect ratios.
- One image has not been heavily cropped before upload.

### The Slider Is Difficult to Use on Mobile

Check:

1. Preview the storefront at a narrow mobile width.
2. Confirm no custom CSS is covering the control.
3. Check whether an app or custom script is interfering with touch behavior.
4. Test in another mobile browser.

### One Image Looks Blurry

Try:

- Uploading a higher-resolution image.
- Re-exporting the image with less compression.
- Using images with similar resolution.

## Recommended Use

The Before & After Slider works best when the comparison adds useful information for the customer.

Use it intentionally rather than adding it purely for decoration.
