# Countdown

Moonlace includes a date-based Countdown section for genuine time-sensitive events and promotions.

The Countdown can be useful for:

- Product launches
- Collection releases
- Sales
- Preorder deadlines
- Seasonal promotions
- Events
- Limited-time offers with a real end date

## Add the Countdown Section

To add a Countdown:

1. Open Shopify admin.
2. Go to **Online Store > Themes**.
3. Find Moonlace and click **Customize**.
4. Open the page or template where you want the Countdown to appear.
5. Click **Add section**.
6. Select the **Countdown** section.
7. Configure the available date, time, text, and link settings.
8. Save.

## Set the End Date

Use the Countdown settings to enter the genuine date and time when the promotion or event ends.

Before publishing, confirm:

- The date is correct.
- The time is correct.
- The timezone matches your intended promotion.
- The supporting text accurately describes the event.

## Add Supporting Content

Depending on the available Moonlace settings, the Countdown may include:

- Heading
- Supporting text
- Button
- Link
- Background
- Promotional message

Keep the message concise and make the action clear.

Examples:

- New Collection Launches Soon
- Sale Ends Friday
- Preorders Close Tonight
- Holiday Release Begins In

## Use Genuine Deadlines

The Moonlace Countdown is intended for legitimate merchant-configured dates.

Do not use the feature to create false urgency.

Avoid:

- Automatically resetting countdowns
- Fake sale deadlines
- Misleading scarcity
- Timers implying an offer expires when it does not
- Repeatedly changing dates only to pressure customers

If a new promotion begins later, configure a new legitimate deadline.

## When the Countdown Ends

After the configured end time passes, the section behavior depends on the available Moonlace settings and current theme version.

Before launching a campaign, test what appears after expiration.

Check:

- Whether the timer reaches zero correctly
- Whether promotional text still makes sense
- Whether any linked offer is still active
- Whether the section should be removed or updated after the event

## Mobile Testing

Preview the Countdown on mobile and confirm:

- Numbers remain readable.
- Labels do not overlap.
- Text wraps correctly.
- Buttons remain accessible.
- No horizontal overflow occurs.
- Background imagery crops appropriately.

## Accessibility

When configuring a Countdown:

- Keep surrounding text readable.
- Do not rely only on color to communicate urgency.
- Use clear headings and labels.
- Make linked buttons descriptive.
- Avoid excessive animation or distracting effects.
- Make sure important offer details are available in normal page text.

## Promotion Accuracy

The Countdown controls presentation only.

Merchants are responsible for ensuring that:

- The advertised promotion is accurate.
- Prices and discounts match the message.
- The end date is genuine.
- Product availability is represented accurately.
- Promotional claims comply with applicable rules and laws.

## Troubleshooting

### The Countdown Shows the Wrong Time

Check:

1. The configured date.
2. The configured time.
3. Your store and promotion timezone.
4. Whether daylight-saving changes affect the intended deadline.
5. That the correct Countdown section is being edited.

### The Countdown Has Already Reached Zero

Confirm the configured end date has not already passed.

If the promotion is over, update or remove the section rather than resetting it without a genuine new event.

### The Countdown Looks Crowded on Mobile

Try:

- Shortening the heading.
- Shortening supporting text.
- Reviewing available spacing settings.
- Using a simpler background.
- Previewing the section at narrow mobile widths.

### The Button Does Not Go to the Correct Page

Check the link configured in the Countdown section and confirm the destination still exists.

## Recommended Use

Use the Countdown when the deadline itself provides useful information to the customer.

A timer should clarify a real event, not manufacture panic because apparently shopping was not stressful enough already.
