# Products & Collections

Moonlace uses Shopify's native product and collection systems to organize and display your catalog.

## Products

Product information is managed through Shopify admin.

To edit a product:

1. Open Shopify admin.
2. Go to **Products**.
3. Select the product you want to edit.
4. Configure the available product details.
5. Save your changes.

Common product information includes:

- Title
- Description
- Media
- Price
- Compare-at price
- Variants
- Inventory
- Product category
- Vendor
- Tags
- Collections
- Online Store availability

Moonlace uses this Shopify data to build the storefront product experience.

## Product Images

Strong product imagery is especially important in Moonlace because the theme is designed for visually driven brands.

For best results:

- Use high-quality images.
- Keep product photography visually consistent.
- Avoid unnecessarily large file sizes.
- Include multiple views when customers benefit from seeing different angles or details.
- Preview product images on both desktop and mobile.
- Avoid placing important details too close to image edges.

## Product Variants

Products can use Shopify variants such as:

- Size
- Color
- Material
- Finish
- Style

After adding or changing variants, test the product page to confirm:

- Variant options are visible.
- Available variants can be selected.
- Unavailable variants are handled correctly.
- Price changes display correctly.
- Add to Cart works.
- Sold-out states display appropriately.

## Product Availability

A product may not appear on your storefront if:

- The product is not active.
- It is not available to the Online Store sales channel.
- Inventory settings prevent purchase.
- The product is not included in the expected collection.
- The product or variant is unavailable.

Review the product settings in Shopify if an item does not appear where expected.

## Product Descriptions

Use product descriptions to provide information customers need before purchasing.

Useful details may include:

- Materials
- Dimensions
- Product care
- Features
- What's included
- Usage information
- Shipping expectations
- Important product-specific notes

Keep descriptions clear and easy to scan.

## Product Media

Depending on Shopify product configuration and supported media types, product media may include:

- Images
- Video
- Other supported Shopify product media

Preview media carefully after adding it to make sure it displays well within your chosen product layout.

## Collections

Collections organize products into groups and help customers browse your store.

Common collection types include:

- New arrivals
- Best sellers
- Jewelry
- Accessories
- Gifts
- Seasonal collections
- Product categories
- Featured products
- Limited releases

Collections are created and managed through Shopify admin.

## Create a Collection

1. Open Shopify admin.
2. Go to **Products > Collections**.
3. Click **Create collection**.
4. Enter the collection title and description.
5. Configure the collection type and product rules.
6. Add an image if desired.
7. Save.

## Manual and Automated Collections

Shopify supports different collection methods.

### Manual Collections

Products are added to the collection individually.

This can be useful for:

- Curated releases
- Gift guides
- Featured products
- Small specialty groups

### Automated Collections

Products are added based on Shopify conditions.

Conditions may use product information such as:

- Tags
- Product type
- Vendor
- Price
- Other supported product conditions

Automated collections can reduce manual maintenance for larger catalogs.

## Collection Images

Collection images may be used in collection headers or promotional sections.

For best results:

- Use high-quality imagery.
- Choose an image that represents the collection clearly.
- Check desktop and mobile cropping.
- Avoid embedding important text directly into the image when possible.

## Collection Descriptions

Use collection descriptions when they provide useful context for shoppers.

Examples include:

- Collection story
- Materials
- Seasonal information
- Gift recommendations
- Product category explanation

Avoid adding long descriptions simply to fill space.

## Filtering

Where enabled and configured through Shopify, Moonlace can display collection filtering controls.

Available filters depend on your store configuration and product data.

Useful filters may include:

- Availability
- Price
- Product type
- Vendor
- Color
- Size
- Other product attributes supported by Shopify

## Sorting

Collection pages can offer sorting options such as:

- Featured
- Best selling
- Alphabetical
- Price
- Date

Available options are controlled by Shopify and the theme configuration.

## Test Collection Pages

After changing collection or product data, test:

- Small collections
- Larger collections
- Sold-out products
- Sale products
- Long product titles
- Long collection titles
- Filtering
- Sorting
- Empty collections
- Mobile layouts

## Product Cards

Moonlace product cards may display information such as:

- Product image
- Title
- Price
- Sale information
- Availability
- Product badges
- Product options where supported

The exact information shown depends on your Moonlace settings and product data.

## Empty Collections

If a collection contains no visible products, review:

1. Product status.
2. Sales-channel availability.
3. Collection conditions.
4. Inventory settings.
5. Manual product assignments.

## Recommended Product Setup

Before launching your storefront:

1. Review every active product.
2. Confirm titles and prices.
3. Confirm variants.
4. Confirm inventory.
5. Review product images.
6. Review descriptions.
7. Confirm collection assignments.
8. Test Add to Cart.
9. Test sold-out products.
10. Preview product and collection pages on mobile.
